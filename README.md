## 🎯 What Does This App Do?

**Nepal_Quake_Watch** combines multiple trusted sources to give you a complete picture of seismic activity and its potential impact. Here is what you can expect:

- **Live Earthquake Monitoring:** See the latest earthquakes reported by the **USGS** (U.S. Geological Survey) and the **NEMRC** (National Earthquake Monitoring and Research Centre) of Nepal. Updates happen automatically.
- **ShakeMap Intensity:** Understand how strongly the ground shakes in different areas, displayed on an easy-to-read map.
- **Population Exposure:** See how many people live in the affected wards (using official census data) so you know the potential scale of impact.
- **Building Vulnerability:** Identify areas where buildings may be more susceptible to damage based on local construction data.
- **Hospitals & Emergency Contacts:** Find the nearest hospitals and get official emergency phone numbers directly from the app.

All of this information is presented on a **simple, interactive map** using Leaflet technology.

## 🗺️ How to Use the App

Using the app is as easy as **look, click, learn**.

- **See Latest Quakes:** When the app opens, you will see a map of Nepal. Recent earthquakes appear as colored circles. The color indicates intensity (red = strongest, green = weakest).
- **Click on a Circle:** Click any circle to see details in a popup window, including magnitude, location, time, and the ShakeMap intensity.
- **View Panels:** On the right side, you will see quick statistics: total earthquakes today, strongest magnitude, and number of affected people in the selected area.
- **Emergency Contacts:** Look at the top bar to find the official emergency numbers for Nepal (Police, Ambulance, Rescue). Keep these close!
- **Find a Hospital:** Use the "Hospitals" button to show hospital locations on the map. Click a hospital icon to see its name and contact number.

That's it! All the data refreshes automatically every few minutes, so you always see the most current information.
